Find your words

Answer a few questions and Tendril drafts a letter, a message, or talking points. Change anything you like; it’s your story. Nothing leaves your device unless you copy it.

Think about safety first

If someone might react with violence, or you depend on them for a home or money, it’s okay to wait. If you do tell them, consider a public place, a supportive person nearby, or a plan for where you could go.

You don’t owe anyone a coming out. Share as much or as little as you want, when you’re ready.
