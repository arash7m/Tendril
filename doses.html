    Good to know

    Tendril helps you remember what you’ve taken. It doesn’t give dosing advice, so follow what your prescriber told you. If you miss a dose and aren’t sure what to do, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

    Websites can’t send reminders while closed. Use Calendar reminder on a medication to add a repeating event to your phone’s calendar instead.
