About Tendril, installing it and your privacy

Use Tendril as an app

Add Tendril to your home screen. It opens full screen, works with no internet connection, and your data stays on your phone. There’s no app store and no account.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button .
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  1. Open your browser’s menu (often ⋮ or …).
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

You’re using the Tendril app. It works offline.

Your data stays with you

Not everyone is out yet, and some people live where being trans isn’t safe. Tendril is built so nobody else can see what you do here.

  • Everything you save is stored in this browser, on this device. There are no accounts and no server copies.
  • Voice practice analyzes your microphone on your device. Audio is never recorded or uploaded.
  • No analytics, ads or trackers.
  • You can turn on an app lock: a PIN that locks Tendril when you leave it and encrypts everything it saves.
  • You can back up your data to a file, protected with a password, and restore it on another device.
  • The Quick exit button, or pressing Esc twice, jumps to a weather search and removes this page from your back button.
