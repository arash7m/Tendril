Voice practice

Shape your resonance

Resonance is how bright or dark your voice sounds, set by the size and shape of the space in your throat and mouth. It changes how your voice comes across as much as pitch does, and it can be trained on its own.

Target zone

The scale runs from typical adult male resonance (0) to typical adult female resonance (100), measured from your voice’s formants. Phone microphones and rooms vary, so treat it as a guide and trust your ears too.

Exercise

    Go easy on your voice

    Resonance work should feel light, never squeezed. If your throat feels tight or sore, stop and rest. Short daily practice works better than long sessions, and a voice coach or speech therapist can help a lot.
