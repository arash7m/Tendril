Go easy on your voice

Practice in short sessions of 10 to 15 minutes and sip water. If your throat feels tight, scratchy or tired, stop and rest. Pushing pitch up by squeezing your throat can strain it; a voice coach or speech therapist can help you find a comfortable technique.
