All measurements

    How to measure
    • Same conditions each time. Morning works well, before eating, in light clothing or none. Stand relaxed and breathe out normally.
    • Keep the tape level all the way around and snug, not tight. A mirror helps, or ask someone you trust.
    • Chest / bust: around the fullest part of your chest.
    • Underbust: just under your chest, where a bra band would sit.
    • Waist: the narrowest part of your torso, usually a little above your belly button.
    • Hips: the widest part of your hips and bum, feet together.
    • Shoulders: all the way around, over the widest part of your shoulders.
    • Thigh and upper arm: the widest part, on the same side each time.
    • Neck: around the middle of your neck.

    Be kind with the numbers

    Bodies change slowly and not in straight lines. Water, hormones, food and time of day all move these numbers a little. Look at the trend over months, not a single day. If tracking ever starts to feel bad, it’s fine to stop or hide a measurement.
